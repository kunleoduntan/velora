# Framework 1.17.5

- Print formats: `money_in_words` now writes the amount in words (for example "One thousand two hundred and fifty naira, forty kobo only"; naira, dollars, pounds, euros, cedis, Kenya shillings and rand are named, other currencies use their code). It was previously the same as the figure.
- Desk: Tax Centre screen and the Tax Management hub entries.
- Released together with VeloraERP 2.100.0: see RELEASE-NOTES-VELORAERP-v2.100.0.md.
