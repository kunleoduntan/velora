# VeloraERP 2.95.2 - fixed or attendance pay, as a policy
- **Pay basis is now a policy**, not a fixed rule. Payroll Automation Settings has a **Default Pay Basis** (Fixed Monthly or Attendance Based) and a **Pay Basis by Staff Category** table (for example Factory Workers = Attendance Based). The Employee has its own **Pay Basis** (Follow Policy / Fixed Monthly / Attendance Based). Order: the employee's own choice, then the staff-category rule, then the company default. You can change any of them at any time; the next Payroll Preparation uses the new setting.
- **Attendance Based** employees get Days Worked and Days in Period filled from approved Attendance: Present, Work From Home, Outdoor Duty, Holiday, Weekly Off and On Leave count a full day, Half Day counts half, Absent counts none. Days with no record count as not worked and are listed. "Only approved attendance counts" can be switched off in the settings.
- **Fixed Monthly** employees are paid the whole period.
- Only salary components marked **Prorate by Attendance** shrink with absence. If an attendance-based employee's structure has none, Needs Attention says so. An attendance-based employee with no attendance at all is left out of the run and listed, not paid zero.
- Checked by tests: policy order (employee, category, default); 25 credited days of 31 pay exactly 25/31 of the prorating component while a fixed employee gets the full amount.

Limits: if an employee is in two categories with different bases, Attendance Based wins. Category effective dates are not consulted. Overtime hours are not pulled from Overtime Requests here (they already flow through their own payroll input). A person who joined mid-period on attendance pay is judged on the days recorded; fixed pay is not prorated for joining date.
