Welcome to Velora Admin

Every organisation runs on quiet work: the request that must reach the right person, the licence that must not expire, the vehicle that must be ready, the visitor who must be welcomed, the contract that must be renewed before the penalty, the document that must be found in seconds. Velora Admin gives that work a proper home.

Requests flow through services with deadlines and approval levels you define; facilities, assets, vehicles, visitors, contracts, vendors, renewals, documents and events are kept as clear records; and the daily jobs watch the calendar so that people do not have to. All of it shares the people, approvals and permissions of the rest of Velora, so the administrative office finally works from the same facts as everyone else.

This guide shows you how to set it up in an afternoon and use it every day.

Other guides on this server: Velora framework · VeloraERP · ProjectOne · Velora Admin. A guide opens only if that application is installed.

1What Velora Admin is

It is the office-administration application of Velora: the place where requests, facilities, assets, vehicles, visitors, contracts, renewals, documents and events are recorded, approved and followed up.

Services and requestsA service catalogue, request forms you design, approval levels, deadlines and a fulfilment checklist for whoever does the work.
Facilities and assetsBuildings and spaces, the equipment in them, maintenance requests and work orders, and office assets with their movements.
Vehicles and travelVehicles with insurance and service dates, trip requests, trips and travel requests.
Visitors and accessPre-registered visitors, check-in and check-out, badges and access cards.
Contracts and renewalsVendors, contracts with renewal notice, and a register of licences, permits and certificates that expire.
Documents and recordsA document register with versions and confidentiality, and incoming and outgoing correspondence with due dates.
Events, risk and complianceEvents with budgets, a risk register, security incidents and compliance requirements with their filing dates.

Everything appears in the App Hub under Administration. Approvals use the Velora workflow engine, so they are drawn on screen and never hard-coded; roles and permissions are the normal Velora ones.

3Set it up in an afternoon

Six small records give everything else its people, places and working days.

RecordWhat it is for
Administration SettingsFiscal year, default currency, week start, working hours, naming series, e-mail and SMS switches and the default escalation days.
Organisation UnitYour departments, divisions and branches, in a tree. Each can have a head and a cost centre.
Office LocationOffices, sites and regions. Facilities, vehicles and visitors point to a location.
PersonAn employee, contractor or other person known to administration, optionally linked to an Employee and a user.
Reporting LineWho reports to whom and from when. It is used to find approvers such as a requester's manager.
Business CalendarWorking days, working hours and holidays, so deadlines count working time only.

Tutorial: first set-up

  1. Open App Hub, choose Administration and open Administration Settings. Fill the fiscal year, currency and working hours; Save.
  2. Create your Organisation Units, starting with the top one and giving each a parent.
  3. Create your Office Locations.
  4. Create a Business Calendar with the working days and your public holidays.
  5. Create a Person for each staff member who will raise or approve requests, then a Reporting Line for each, so approvers can be found.

4Services and requests

A Request Type says what can be asked for; a Service says how it is delivered; a Request is one person asking.

RecordWhat it is for
Request TypeA kind of request (for example Vehicle Request, Office Supplies). It carries the deadline in hours, the default department, whether a cost is needed, and its default Approval Matrix.
ServiceAn entry in your service catalogue with its code, category, team, deadline, instructions and whether evidence is required. A Service can have its own request form fields (Service Form Field).
Fulfilment TemplateThe checklist of tasks to perform for a Service, in order, with who does each and which need evidence.
RequestOne request: who asks, what type, priority (Low to Urgent), required-by date, cost estimate and description. Status runs Draft, Pending Approval, Approved or Rejected, In Progress, Completed or Cancelled.
Request FulfilmentThe work after approval: assignee, team, due date, tasks and progress. Status runs Unassigned to Closed, with Blocked when something is in the way.
Service FeedbackThe requester's rating once the work is done.

Tutorial: raise and complete a request

  1. Open Request Type, create Office Supplies, give it a deadline in hours and choose an Approval Matrix (section 4).
  2. Open Service, create the service, choose the request type and the team that delivers it.
  3. Optionally open Fulfilment Template, link the service and add the tasks.
  4. As a staff member, open Request, choose New, pick the type, describe what you need and Save. Submit it for approval.
  5. The approver sees it in their approval inbox and approves or rejects with a comment.
  6. After approval the request is fulfilled: assign the work, tick tasks off, then complete it. The requester can leave feedback.

5Approvals, delegation and escalation

Who must approve a request depends on its type, department and cost. You decide that on screen.

RecordWhat it is for
Approval MatrixA named set of levels. It can apply to one request type or department.
Approval Matrix LevelOne level: the approver is a role or a named user, with an optional minimum and maximum amount, so small requests stop at level 1 and large ones go further.
Approval DelegationA person hands their approvals to someone else between two dates, for example while on leave.
Approval Escalation RuleIf an approval is not done within the stated hours, notify an escalation user or role.

Delegation and escalation are checked when an approval is acted on and by the daily job (section 11).

6Facilities and assets

Know what you have, where it is and when it needs attention.

RecordWhat it is for
Facility and Facility SpaceBuildings, floors and rooms in a tree, with type, capacity and manager.
Facility AssetEquipment in a space (lift, generator, air conditioner) with serial number, warranty expiry and maintenance frequency in days. The next maintenance date follows from the last one.
Maintenance RequestA fault or service need for a facility asset, with priority, assignee, vendor and resolution notes.
Facility Work OrderPlanned or ad hoc work: scheduled and completed dates, estimated and actual cost, optionally repeating.
Office Asset and Office Asset MovementFurniture, laptops and similar assets, who holds them and every hand-over, with dates and remarks.

7Vehicles, trips and travel

Keep vehicles legal and available, and trips accountable.

RecordWhat it is for
VehicleRegistration, make, model, driver, location, insurance and roadworthiness expiry, last and next service and the service interval.
Trip RequestA person asks for a vehicle: pick-up, destination, start and end, then the vehicle and driver assigned. Status runs Requested, Assigned, In Progress, Completed or Cancelled; odometer readings are kept at the start and end.
TripThe journey record, with passengers, estimated and actual cost.
Travel RequestA traveller's trip away: destination, dates, transport, accommodation, visa needs and estimated cost.

8Visitors and access cards

A visitor is expected, welcomed and recorded.

RecordWhat it is for
VisitorName, organisation, host, purpose, expected date, ID type and number, vehicle number and photo. Status runs Pre-Registered, Checked In, Checked Out or No Show, with the check-in time recorded.
Access CardA card number, its type and status, where it is used and who holds it, with issue and return dates.

Tutorial: receive a visitor

  1. The host opens Visitor, chooses New and fills the visitor and the expected date (Pre-Registered).
  2. At the gate, open the record, give a badge number and record the check-in (Checked In).
  3. On leaving, record the check-out (Checked Out). If the visitor never came, mark No Show.

9Vendors, contracts and renewals

Nothing should expire by surprise.

RecordWhat it is for
Vendor Category, Vendor and Vendor Administration ProfileWho you buy services from, with contact, tax ID, qualification status and performance rating. The profile can link a Vendor to its ERP Supplier.
ContractTitle, vendor, type (Service Agreement, Supply Agreement, Lease, Consulting, Maintenance, Other), value, start and end dates, renewal notice days and whether it renews automatically. Status runs Draft, Active, Expiring Soon, Expired or Terminated.
Contract RenewalEach renewal of a contract, with the old and new end date.
Renewal RegisterLicences, permits, certificates, insurance, subscriptions, vehicle documents and facility certifications: the item, the authority, expiry date, lead days, responsible officer, cost and status (Active, Renewal In Progress, Renewed, Expired, Cancelled).

Use the Renewal Register for anything with an expiry date that is not a contract. The daily renewal scan (section 11) looks at every Active or Renewal In Progress entry.

10Documents and correspondence

Find any document in seconds and know which version is current.

RecordWhat it is for
Document CategoryGroups of documents; can require an expiry date or periodic review, with a default review frequency.
Document Register and Document VersionOne register entry per document with owner, department, confidentiality and the current file; each new file is a numbered version with change notes.
CorrespondenceIncoming and outgoing letters and messages: subject, parties, reference number, priority, handler, date and response due date, with a link to the related document.

11Events

Plan an event once, with its money.

RecordWhat it is for
EventTitle, type, organiser, start and end, internal space or external venue, expected attendees and status.
Event Budget LineEach cost with its vendor, estimated and actual amount, so the event's budget against actual is visible.

12Risk, security and compliance

Name the risk, record the incident, meet the filing date.

RecordWhat it is for
Risk RegisterRisk with category, probability, impact and the resulting score, owner, treatment, mitigation plan and review date.
Security IncidentWhat happened, when and where, severity, who reported it, resolution notes and an optional link to the risk it relates to.
Compliance RequirementA regulatory obligation: the body, frequency, responsible person, next due date, last filed date and the evidence document.

13Supplies, knowledge, tasks and checklists

The small records that keep the office moving.

RecordWhat it is for
Supply Item and Stock TransactionConsumables with quantity on hand, reorder level and unit cost; each issue or receipt is a transaction.
Knowledge ArticleHelp articles for requesters, tied to a service or category and published when ready, with a review date.
TaskFollow-up work from any source, with priority, assignee, due date and optional repeat.
Checklist Template and Person ChecklistA reusable list (for example joining or leaving) applied to a Person, with each task tracked to completion.

14What runs by itself every day

Velora Admin registers three daily jobs with the Velora scheduler.

RecordWhat it is for
Service maintenanceChecks request deadlines and approval escalations.
Admin automationRuns your active Automation Rule records (a trigger event, optional conditions and actions).
Renewal scanRecords upcoming expiries from the Renewal Register for the framework's notification rules.

The scan records renewal events but does not itself send e-mail. To be told by e-mail or in the app, define a notification rule in the framework's Notification Engine. The scheduler must be running on the server (the installer sets this up).

15Questions people ask

QuestionAnswer
I cannot see Administration in the App Hub.Your role needs permission on at least one Velora Admin document. Ask your administrator.
A request is stuck at Pending Approval.Open it and check its Approval Matrix level. The approver may be on leave: use an Approval Delegation, or ask for the matrix to be corrected.
Can approval steps be changed without a developer?Yes. Approval Matrix levels and workflows are set on screen.
Where do I find how to install or upgrade?In the Velora framework manual at /tutorial on your server.
