# VeloraERP Commercial Controls — v0.18.0 to v0.21.0

## 1. Sales Order → Sales Invoice

The converter creates a draft Sales Invoice only. It does not submit the invoice or create a Receipt. Each invoice line stores `source_line`, while the invoice header stores `source_doctype=Sales Order` and `source_document=<order>`. The source order is locked while conversion quantity is calculated. Draft and submitted invoices reserve order quantity; cancelled invoices are ignored.

For stock-managed Inventory Items, invoiceable quantity is capped by submitted Delivery Note quantities. Delivered quantities are normalized into the Sales Order line UOM using the source and delivery conversion factors. Service/non-stock lines remain order-billable. Server-side invoice validation repeats the quantity and source checks, so a client-side/API bypass is not sufficient to over-invoice.

Sales Order lines require a Revenue Account before conversion. Rate, line discounts, tax account/code/rate and source line are preserved and revalidated. The current inventory cost floor is still checked at Sales Invoice validation; a price that was valid when ordered may be blocked if current cost has risen.

## 2. Credit exposure

`receivables_exposure` derives customer exposure from the customer Receivables Control Account ledger in base currency. `open_order_commitment` adds the remaining value of submitted Sales Orders after subtracting quantities already represented by draft or submitted Sales Invoices. Cancelled invoices do not reserve quantities. Exchange rates convert transaction values to company base currency.

Credit Limit zero means unlimited. Credit Hold blocks independently and requires a reason. Credit enforcement can be disabled centrally in Finance Settings; if enabled, the Sales Order and Sales Invoice submit hooks call the same service. The exposure scan has explicit row limits and fails closed rather than silently calculating from a truncated population.

## 3. Realised margin

On Delivery Note stock issue, the inventory engine obtains the valuation rate/issue cost from FIFO layers or the configured moving/weighted valuation method. For Delivery Notes it writes `issue_cost_rate` and `issue_cost_amount` on the corresponding Delivery Note Line. The Sales Margin Analysis report uses this actual issue valuation when submitted delivery coverage is at least the invoice line quantity. If coverage is incomplete, it retains the invoice-time cost snapshot and labels it as an estimate.

For periodic Weighted Average, the final period-close adjustment may still change period COGS; the report uses the issue-time value recorded at delivery and does not recalculate closed-period allocations. Sales Returns are not yet netted from the original margin.

## 4. Sales Returns and complaints

The Sales Invoice → Sales Return converter locks the source invoice, calculates remaining return quantities from active return documents, and creates a draft return. The existing Sales Return controller validates the return amounts, tax reversals, source line, account and cumulative quantities again.

Restock returns can require a passed quality inspection and inspection reference. Quarantine returns require a warehouse flagged `is_quarantine_warehouse`. The inventory movement is posted to the selected return warehouse; quarantine warehouse access should be restricted by role.

Customer Complaints are auditable records linked to customers and optionally to submitted invoices/delivery notes. The complaint lifecycle is constrained; resolving/closing requires a resolution and date. Linking a Sales Return or replacement Sales Order does not automatically submit either transaction.

## 5. Security and integrity assumptions

- All quantity, pricing, cost-floor, credit and return caps are checked server-side.
- Source headers are locked during quantity reservation/conversion.
- Financial postings remain immutable and are generated by the existing posting engine on invoice/return submission.
- Operational documents do not create revenue postings merely because they are converted.
- The release does not add a general-purpose override that permits below-cost sales or credit-limit breaches.
