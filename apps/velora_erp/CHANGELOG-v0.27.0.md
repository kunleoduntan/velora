# Changelog — VeloraERP 0.27.0

## Added
- Fixed Asset Transfer with approval, stale-source protection and immutable movement history.
- Fixed Asset History Log and trusted event writer.
- Fixed Asset Reconciliation script report.
- Asset identifiers on acquisition ledger postings for Fixed Asset Purchase and capitalised Purchase Invoice.

## Changed
- Fixed Asset Purchase acquisition cost and residual value are stored in company base currency.
- Fixed Assets workspace includes transfers, history and GL reconciliation.
