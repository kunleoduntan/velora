# VeloraERP v0.69.0 — Receivables and payables ageing and collection prioritisation

- As-of-date ageing with Not Due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and Over 90 day buckets.
- Separate currency totals; no invalid cross-currency aggregation.
- Customer and Supplier ageing read models accept outstanding amounts net of approved allocations.
- Dunning and payment follow-up candidate generation with dispute exclusions and amount thresholds.
- No automatic emails, reminders or collection actions are sent.
