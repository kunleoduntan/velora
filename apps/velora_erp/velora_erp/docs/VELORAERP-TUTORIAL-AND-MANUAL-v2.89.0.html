1What VeloraERP is

VeloraERP is an app that runs on the Velora framework: one integrated core for finance, sales, purchasing, inventory, fixed assets, budgets, payroll, human resources, investments and customer support. Version 2.89.0 on Velora 1.13.0.

Design rules that run through the product

  • One ledger. Every financial document posts through the Financial Posting Engine into the append-only Account Ledger. Documents never write ledger rows themselves.
  • Approval is a workflow you draw, never code. Who approves differs from one company to the next, so no document is blocked by a built-in approval. If no workflow is switched on for a document it simply saves and submits; when one is on, it alone decides who may do what (see "Roles and approval workflows").
  • Cancel and amend, as in ERPNext. A submitted document is never edited. To correct it you cancel it (it disappears from the ledgers) and amend it into a new draft, or duplicate it, then submit the right version. A cancel log keeps who, when, why and what was removed.
  • Company-scoped. Every company has its own database and its own chart, parties, stock and people.
  • Simple security. Roles per company; sensitive items such as employee relations are restricted to HR Manager and System Manager.

3First-day setup: the right order

Set up in this order and every later step has what it needs.

  1. Finance Settings. Country, legal entity, base currency, accounting basis and framework, financial-year start month, number and decimal format, backdated posting rules, closed-period protection, exchange-rate source, tax rounding, change tracking, reasons for overrides, customer credit limit enforcement, loan deduction limits.
  2. Financial Year and Financial Periods. Create the year and its periods. Posting to a closed period is blocked when the setting is on.
  3. Account Types, Account Groups and Accounts. The 33 default account types are created for you (Bank, Cash, Receivable, Inventory, Fixed Asset, Payable, Tax Payable, Withholding Tax, Revenue, Investment, Accrued Income, Tax Receivable, Deferred Income and so on). Build the chart of accounts and mark control accounts. Import it with the data importer if you have it in Excel.
  4. Dimensions. Cost Centres, Business Units, Branches, Projects and Accounting Dimensions with Dimension Values, if you analyse by them.
  5. Currencies and rates. Currency, Currency Exchange Rate (only needed for foreign currency).
  6. Bank Accounts. One per bank or cash account, linked to a Bank or Cash type ledger account.
  7. Customer Groups, Customers, Supplier Groups, Suppliers. Each with its control account, credit limit and terms (Payment Terms).
  8. Inventory. Inventory Settings (asset, COGS, GRNI, adjustment, variance accounts), Warehouses, Inventory Items (valuation method chosen before any movement), Opening Stock Entry.
  9. Fixed asset categories with depreciation books, if you hold assets.
  10. Opening balances. Opening Balance Entry, per-party opening invoices, Opening Stock Entry, asset opening balances.
  11. People. Departments, Designations, Branches, Work Locations, Employees; then payroll tax jurisdiction and rules, salary components and structures.
  12. Roles and approval workflows. Create roles, grant them, then draw an approval workflow for each document that needs one (next section). Skip this and documents simply post without approval.
  13. Naming Series Rules for document prefixes if the defaults do not suit.
  14. Notifications. Configure email (and SMS or WhatsApp if wanted) in notification settings and send a test.
The Setup Readiness check (velora readiness and the ERP configuration provider) lists settings still missing.

4Roles and approval workflows

Roles that ship with the data model

System Manager, Finance Manager, Accounts User, HR Manager and the other roles named in each record type's permissions. Add roles to suit your organisation; roles are per company. Sensitive records (Employee Relations cases) are visible to HR Manager and System Manager only. Employee self-service shows a person only their own records, found from the login's linked Employee, never from an ID sent by the browser.

Approval is a workflow you draw

VeloraERP has no approval rule written in code. A company decides, per document, whether anything needs approving and by whom:

  • No workflow switched on: the document saves and submits straight away. Nothing is blocked. A document whose status is left at Pending is settled automatically.
  • A workflow switched on: it alone decides. It can name roles or particular people, ask for one, any, all or a number of approvers, test conditions such as the amount, run groups in parallel (Finance and Operations both sign off) and refuse the creator as approver ("the creator cannot approve").

Build one under Settings → Workflows; tutorial 2 in section 5 walks through it. A company can have a different workflow for every document type.

The few powers that are not document approvals

Seven actions are not a step in any document's life, so they have their own list under Finance Settings → Approvals ("Other powers"): override a budget block, approve a scheduled report, submit / review / approve publication / publish a reporting case, and approve an HR policy version. Each row lists the roles that hold the power. Empty means anyone whose role permissions allow the action; nothing is restricted until you type roles in. The built-in Administrator always keeps every power, so a wrong setting can never lock a company out.

Only fill a row when you really want to narrow a power, for example let only your Chief Accountant publish a reporting case.

5Guided tutorials

Ten walkthroughs, each about ten minutes. Do them in order the first time; afterwards use them as a reminder. Screen names are exactly as they appear in the Desk.

Practise in a test company first. In every tutorial “submit” means the Submit button on the document, which posts it.

Tutorial 1: Set up a company in one sitting

  1. Open Finance Settings and fill country, base currency, accounting basis, financial-year start month and number format. Save.
  2. Create the Financial Year with its periods.
  3. Build the chart: Account Groups then Accounts. Mark Bank, Cash, Receivable and Payable accounts with the right account type. Using Excel? Use the data importer (section 29).
  4. Create a Bank Account linked to a Bank or Cash ledger account.
  5. Create a Customer and a Supplier. Give each its control account and (for the customer) a credit limit.
  6. Open Inventory Settings and set the inventory, GRNI, cost of goods sold, stock adjustment and purchase price variance accounts. Create a Warehouse and one Inventory Item with a valuation method (this cannot be changed after the first movement).
  7. Run the Setup Readiness check; every item it lists is something still missing.

Tutorial 2: Draw an approval workflow (no code)

  1. Go to Settings → Workflows and choose New. Pick the document, for example Purchase Invoice.
  2. Under Start from choose a preset: One approver, Two levels, Parallel sign-off or By amount. The states and the buttons appear as plain sentences: “From Draft, action Send for approval, to Pending Approval”.
  3. For each step choose who may act: anyone, one or more role chips (Finance Manager, Chief Accountant), or named people. Choose how many must agree (one, any, all, or a number).
  4. Optional: add a condition such as Grand Total is more than 5,000,000 so only large documents need the second approver, or add parallel groups (Finance and Operations).
  5. Read the automatic diagram and the problems list; fix anything it flags. Switch the workflow on and save.
  6. Create a Purchase Invoice: the action buttons now follow your workflow. Switch the workflow off and the same document saves and submits freely again.
Because approval is only ever a workflow, you can give two companies, or two document types, completely different rules without any programming.

Tutorial 3: Buy goods, from order to supplier payment, merging orders

  1. Create two Purchase Orders to the same supplier in the same currency (say 10 and 5 units of item A) and submit both. Their Received % and Billed % are 0 and the status is Open.
  2. Create a new Purchase Receipt. Click Get Items From → Purchase Order. Tick both orders and click Add. The supplier, currency and warehouse fill in and 15 units appear, each line tagged with its order.
  3. Change a quantity if only part arrived (say 8 of the first line). Save and submit. Stock increases and GRNI is recorded.
  4. Open the first order: Received % is now partial and the status is Partially Processed. The second order shows 100% received.
  5. Create a Purchase Invoice. Click Get Items From → Purchase Receipt, tick the receipt and add. Check the tax lines and submit. GRNI clears and the supplier is credited.
  6. Receive the remaining 2 units on a second receipt (Get Items From again: the order offers exactly the open 2). Invoice it the same way. The orders now show 100% / 100% and become Completed.
  7. Pay: create a Payment with purpose Supplier Payment and allocate it to the invoices. Check Supplier Statement.
Try to receive 20 units against the 10-unit order: the system refuses and tells you which order and item are over.

Tutorial 4: Buy without a receipt, or receive without an order

  1. Services or expenses: create a Purchase Invoice and use Get Items From → Purchase Order, or simply type the lines. No receipt is needed for non-stock items.
  2. Goods that arrive with no order: create a Purchase Receipt directly. Pick the supplier, warehouse and items; the item description fills from the item if you leave it blank. Submit.
  3. Invoice it later from Get Items From → Purchase Receipt.

Tutorial 5: Sell, deliver and collect, merging orders

  1. Submit two Sales Orders to one customer, same currency.
  2. Create a Delivery Note, click Get Items From → Sales Order, tick both, add. Adjust quantities, save and submit. Stock reduces at cost; Delivered % rises on each order.
  3. Create a Sales Invoice and choose Get Items From → Delivery Note. Tick the delivery; its lines come across at the order prices with the right revenue and tax accounts. Submit.
  4. Or bill several orders in one invoice with Get Items From → Sales Order. Stock lines can be billed only up to what has been delivered.
  5. Create a Receipt with purpose Customer Receipt and allocate it to the invoice. If the customer withheld tax add a withholding line.
  6. Open the Customer Statement. The orders show Billed 100% and are Completed.

Tutorial 6: Check customer credit with the Customer Credit Exposure report

  1. Open Reports → Customer Credit Exposure. It opens already run with its default filters.
  2. Filters: Customer (one customer, or leave empty for all); Only Over Limit (a tick box, off by default; tick it to see only customers over limit or on hold); Include Open Sales Orders (a tick box, on by default; untick it to look at receivables alone).
  3. Press Run after changing a filter. Columns show receivables, open-order commitment, total exposure, credit limit, available credit and status (Within Limit, Over Limit, On Hold or Unlimited; a limit of zero means unlimited).
  4. Export to Excel downloads exactly what is on screen. Print prints the report sheet without the menus.
  5. To stop an over-limit customer being invoiced, turn on Enforce customer credit limit in Finance Settings.

Tutorial 7: Make a form fit your business (Customize Form)

  1. Open Settings → Customize Form (visible to administrators and designers) and choose a record type, say Customer.
  2. For any field you may change the label, make it required, hidden or read-only, and decide whether it shows in the list and in the filter bar.
  3. Click Add field, choose its type (Data, Select, Link, Date, Check and so on) and the field it should appear after. Save; the form shows it at once.
  4. Remove a custom field you added by mistake with its remove button. Fields that ship with the product can be hidden but not deleted.

Tutorial 8: Tables that work like a spreadsheet

  1. Open any document with lines. A table starts with the six most useful columns (item, name, quantity, rate, amount, unit or warehouse).
  2. Click Columns to add a hidden column such as the source order, to reorder, or to widen a column. Your choice is remembered for you.
  3. Colours help the eye: pink cells are links (item, account), light blue cells are amounts, light yellow cells are plain data. Rows are not striped; the selected cell is outlined.
  4. Link fields search like Frappe: type any part of a name or code and the best matches come first, with a short description next to each.

Tutorial 9: Month-end in ten steps

  1. Submit or deliberately leave in draft every document for the month.
  2. Reconcile each bank account in Bank Reconciliation.
  3. Post depreciation, accruals and recognition; run stock reconciliation if you counted.
  4. Open Controls Check and clear every receivable and payable finding.
  5. Review the ledger in GL Review.
  6. Check the order fulfilment: orders stuck at Partially Processed are goods not yet received, delivered or billed.
  7. Prepare the Financial Period Close checklist with evidence, approve (through your workflow, if any) and submit; the period is Closed.
  8. Print the statements from the Financial Reporting Centre.

Tutorial 10: Upgrade safely and keep the database fast

  1. Back up: velora backup --all.
  2. Stop the service, install the new wheels, then run velora upgrade and start the service again. The upgrade patches (for example the one that fills order Received / Delivered / Billed %) run once, automatically.
  3. If the Update Centre left maintenance mode on, switch it off with python3 -m velora --workspace /srv/velora/workspace maintenance off.
  4. Open Update Centre → Database speed, review the preview and press Tune the database now. It adds missing indexes, refreshes statistics and reclaims space; it never changes your data. Do it after big imports and every few months.
  5. Hard-refresh the browser so it loads the new Desk.

6The posting engine and the Account Ledger

The single most important idea in VeloraERP finance.

PartRole
Financial Posting Batch / LineThe engine's own working record of one posting (source document, date, period, currency, totals). Internal: it is not shown in the Desk menu.
Account Ledger RecordThe account-level record (what the ledger reports read). Created only by the engine; nobody edits it. It is removed only when the document that produced it is cancelled.

What the engine guarantees

  • Each batch balances (debits equal credits in the base currency).
  • Each line has exactly one positive debit or credit.
  • Control accounts carry the right party; closed periods are refused (when protected); budget control is applied to expense increases.
  • Idempotency: retrying the same source event returns the existing batch; the same key with different content is refused.
  • Atomic: batch and ledger records are written together or not at all.
  • Cancel: cancelling the source document removes its ledger rows in the same transaction (see below). There are no reversing entries to read around.

What this means for you

  • A submitted document cannot be edited. Fix it by Cancel and then Amend (a new draft copy linked to the cancelled one) or Duplicate.
  • Every figure on a report traces back to a ledger record and from there to a source document.
  • Account balances are always the sum of posted records; there is no separate balance to get out of step.

Cancelling a posted document

Press Cancel on a submitted document. You need the cancel right on that document type (any role that may submit it has it by default; change it under Settings → Roles). The dialog asks for a reason and shows what depends on the document.

  • Open periods only. A document posted in a closed Financial Year or Period cannot be cancelled.
  • Other submitted documents that depend on it stop the cancel, and the dialog names them: a Sales Order with a Delivery Note, a Delivery Note with an invoice, a Purchase Receipt with a Landed Cost Voucher. Press Cancel All to cancel the whole chain, newest first, in one go; if any step fails, nothing is cancelled.
  • Things the document owns are cleaned up for you. Cancel an invoice and the receipts or payments that settled it stay, but become unapplied money on the customer or supplier. Cancel a receipt or payment and the invoices it settled are open again. Prepayment and deferred-income schedules made from the document, intercompany allocations and fixed assets that were only just created from a purchase invoice are removed with it; assets that already have depreciation or other history refuse the cancel and say which.
  • Stock. Cancelling a stock document removes its stock ledger rows and recalculates the running balances after it. If later documents already used that stock (a delivery after a receipt), the cancel is refused and names them. Costs of issues already made are not recalculated; the stock value and the Inventory account still agree.
  • Evidence. The document stays on record as Cancelled. A Posting Cancellation Log keeps who cancelled it, when, why, what was unlinked and a copy of the ledger and stock rows that were removed (Finance Manager, Accountant and Auditor can read it).
  • Amend or Duplicate on the cancelled document opens a new draft with the same values; correct it and submit it. The amendment is named after the original (SI-0001 → SI-0001-1).

Cancelling the other families

Every document type can be cancelled with the same button and the same rules. Documents that change other records when they are submitted (a depreciation run changing an asset, a payroll run changing loans) save a copy of those records just before submit, and cancel puts them back exactly as they were.

DocumentWhat cancel does
Fixed Asset Depreciation Run, Revaluation, Impairment, Disposal, Transfer, Component Adjustment, CGU Impairment and Reversal, Lease Modification and TerminationRemoves the ledger rows, deletes the asset history entries of that document and restores the asset (cost, accumulated depreciation, carrying amount, status, components, book balances, lease schedule) to its values before it. If a later event touched the same asset, the cancel is refused and names it: cancel the newest first.
Fixed Asset Purchase, LeaseRemoves the asset(s) it created, if they have had no later activity; a purchase request is open again.
Payroll PaymentSalary slips go back to unpaid (or the earlier part-paid amount).

Employee Loan, Employee AdvanceCan be cancelled while nothing has been repaid, recovered or settled; otherwise cancel the repayments or settlements first.
Employee Loan Repayment, Advance SettlementThe loan schedule or advance balance goes back.

7Chart of accounts and dimensions

  • Account Type decides behaviour (root type Asset, Liability, Income, Expense, Equity; normal balance; whether party tracking is needed; whether cash-like).
  • Account Group and Account build the tree. Control accounts (Receivable, Payable, Payroll Payable, Loan Receivable) require a party on every line.
  • Cost Centre, Business Unit, Branch, Project and user-defined Accounting Dimensions with Dimension Values tag every line for analysis and budgets.
  • Cost Distribution Rule and Finance Settings' cost distribution lines split shared costs across centres by percentage; Intercompany Cost Allocation records charges across companies.
  • Account Statement Mapping and Financial Statement Row map accounts into statement lines (see Financial reporting).
Open Account Ledger and Trial Balance reports first after loading a chart; if the trial balance is empty, nothing has posted yet.

Copying and merging documents (Get Items From)

Open a draft Delivery Note or Sales Invoice and use Get Items From. A picker lists the submitted documents that still have something to take: Sales Orders for a Delivery Note; Sales Orders or Delivery Notes for a Sales Invoice. Tick one or several (the first one fixes the customer; the rest must be the same customer and currency) and the open lines are added. Quantity offered is what is still open: ordered minus what other documents already took. You can edit quantities before saving, remove lines, or type extra lines by hand.

  • Every line remembers the document and line it came from, so merging three orders into one delivery still credits each order correctly.
  • You cannot deliver or bill more than is open on a source line; the message names the order and item.
  • Stock items are billed from a Sales Order only up to what has been delivered. Billing a Delivery Note bills exactly what it delivered.
  • The classic Create button on an order or delivery still works for the single-document case.

Credit control

Set a credit limit on the Customer and turn on Enforce customer credit limit in Finance Settings. Exposure = receivables ledger balance (+ open sales orders if included). A hold or over-limit invoice is refused on the server. Report: Customer Credit Exposure (tutorial 6 in section 5).

Selling price rules

Inventory Settings can compute price from cost: selling cost basis, default margin percent, rounding step, block selling below cost, require cost for stock sales, and whether staff may type a price manually or the price is strict.

Purchase Order → Purchase Receipt → Purchase Invoice → Payment, the way ERPNext does it. You may skip any step, start any document by hand, or merge several documents into one.

Copying and merging documents (Get Items From)

On a draft Purchase Receipt or Purchase Invoice use Get Items From:

  • Purchase Receipt from Purchase Orders: tick several orders of one supplier and currency; each open line comes across with its open quantity.
  • Purchase Invoice from Purchase Orders (services, or goods not received yet; goods already received are billed from their receipt instead) or from Purchase Receipts (each invoice line is matched to the receipt line, so GRNI clears exactly).

When the receipt or invoice is submitted, every source order is updated: Received % and Billed % rise, and an order that is fully received and billed becomes Completed. Cancel the receipt or invoice and the figures go back. Receiving or billing more than the order allows is refused, naming the order and the item. Differences between receipt and invoice price post to the purchase price variance account. Inventory Settings must have the GRNI account set before stock can be received.

GRNI matching and the stock transfer logic are described in the v0.13 document set.

10Cash, bank and reconciliation

Receipts and Payments

Distinct submittable documents. The bank or cash account is debited (receipt) or credited (payment) by the net amount; other lines (withholding, bank charges, differences) sit on the opposite side so cash is never falsified. Cash accounts must be active Asset accounts of type BANK or CASH.

Receipt purposesPayment purposes
Customer Receipt, Customer Refund Recovery, Other Income, Supplier Refund, Loan Received, Capital Introduced, Tax Refund, Customer Advance, Deferred Income, Employee Repayment, Other ReceiptSupplier Payment, Expense Payment, Customer Refund, Employee Reimbursement, Employee Advance, Loan Repayment, Asset Acquisition, Prepayment, Supplier Advance, Tax Payment, Other Payment

Worked example, customer owes 1,000, withholds tax 50, pays 950: Debit Bank 950, Debit WHT Receivable 50, Credit Customer Receivables 1,000. For a supplier: Debit Supplier Payables 1,000, Credit WHT Payable 50, Credit Bank 950.

Vouchers and batches

  • Financial Voucher and Journal Entry Voucher: manual postings (add an approval workflow if you want sign-off).
  • Expense Batch: many expense payments in one document.
  • Batch Bank Transaction: many receipts or payments through one bank movement, importable from Excel or CSV.
  • Bank Transaction Processor with Bank Transaction Coding Rules: imported bank lines are coded to accounts automatically by rules (text, amount, direction) and posted after review.

Bank reconciliation centre (#/bank-reconciliation)

  1. Import the statement (CSV, Excel and other supported parsers; map columns once and reuse).
  2. Auto-match statement lines to ledger records by amount, date window and reference; matches are Suggested.
  3. Review: confirm or reject suggestions; unmatch; match manually many-to-many with a reason; ignore lines with a reason (and restore).
  4. Code and post lines that have no ledger record (bank charges, interest) using coding rules.
  5. Compute and lock the reconciliation for the period: opening, closing, cleared items, differences. A locked group cannot be changed.

A scheduled hourly job can auto-match for banks that opt in. The reconciliation is an evidenced control in period close.

Open item: folding Bank Reconciliation, Bank Statement Line and matches into one doctype; and "create" buttons on Bank Transaction Processor, Batch Bank Transaction and Expense Batch.

12Customers, suppliers and party ledgers

  • Every party has a control account; all postings to it carry the party, so the sub-ledger always agrees with the control account.
  • Reports: Customer Ledger, Customer Statement, Supplier Ledger, Supplier Statement, Employee Ledger, Customer Credit Exposure, ageing.
  • Advances: customer advances sit in a party-tracked liability account, supplier advances in a party-tracked asset account; they are allocated to invoices later.
  • Subledger reconciliation proves each control account equals the sum of party balances and lists differences.
  • Sending statements: use the Broadcast Centre with the Customer document type as the audience.

13Tax and multi-currency

  • Tax engine: tax codes on invoice lines, inclusive or exclusive pricing, rounding method from Finance Settings.
  • Withholding tax: posted to Withholding Tax accounts on receipts and payments (see Cash and bank) and on investment income (receivable credit by default, or final-tax expense).
  • Deferred tax: Deferred Tax Assessment and a Deferred Tax Posting Inbox for review before posting; period close refuses to close while items remain pending.
  • Tax refund credits: reviewed through the Tax Refund Credit Review.
  • Payroll tax: jurisdictions, rules with bands, statutory deductions summary report.
  • Multi-currency: transaction currency and exchange rate on documents; base-currency amounts on every ledger record; Currency Exchange Rate with source and rate type.
Open item: a dedicated VAT input/output report is not built yet; use the Account Ledger report for the tax accounts meanwhile.

15Inventory and valuation

Valuation per item (choose before the first movement)

MethodHow issues are costed

16Budgets and budget control

Financial Budget (Budget Workbench, #/budgets)

A budget has accounts, lines by month, allocations to dimensions (cost centre, branch) by percentage or method, and scenarios (for example base, optimistic, pessimistic). The planner helps: project from last year's actuals with growth, spread annual totals over months, split by allocation table, compare scenarios.

Budget Adjustment

Approved transfers and increases are added to the effective budget without editing the original (authority: budget.adjust).

Budget control (company setting finance.budget_control)

{"mode": "Off" | "Warn" | "Block",
 "budget_name": "FY2027 Operating Budget",
 "control_period": "Month" | "YTD" | "Year",
 "tolerance_percent": 0,
 "unbudgeted": "Allow" | "Warn" | "Block"}
  • Default is Off; nothing changes until you opt in.
  • Only the increase of expense spend is controlled; income, balance-sheet and reversing postings are not.

Tutorial: set a monthly cap

  1. Create and approve the Financial Budget for the year.
  2. Set budget control to Warn first; watch for a month.
  3. Move to Block with a 5% tolerance and decide who holds the override role.

17Period close and year-end

Draft preparation → checklist and evidence review → approval (if you have a workflow) → submit → period Closed.

Automated gates

  1. Base-currency debits and credits posted up to the period end must agree.
  2. No Deferred Tax Posting Inbox item dated up to the period end may be pending review or approved but unposted.

Evidenced manual controls

Bank and cash reconciliation, receivables, payables, inventory valuation, fixed assets and depreciation, current and deferred tax, accruals and prepayments, suspense and clearing accounts, revenue and expense cut-off, disclosures and schedules. Evidence references are mandatory for bank, receivables, payables, inventory, fixed asset and tax items. Approved checklist content is fingerprinted; a manager must reset approval before changing it.

  • A period cannot simply be set to Closed; only a submitted Financial Period Close closes it, and that record cannot be cancelled or deleted.
  • Year End Close moves the year's income and expense to retained earnings through its own checklist.
  • A period reopening workflow is not included in this release.

18Controls Check and General Ledger Review

Receivables and Payables Controls Check (#/controls-check, GET /api/controls-check)

Read-only checks an auditor or financial controller would ask for. Each finding lists the documents so you can open them; nothing is changed or posted. Run it before month-end and clear the findings.

General Ledger Review (#/gl-review, GET /api/gl-review)

Period-against-period analytical review. You choose the period, a prior period that does not overlap it, a percentage threshold (default 50) and a minimum amount (default 10,000). It flags:

  • Large movements against the earlier period.
  • New accounts with significant activity, and accounts active before but silent now.
  • Accounts with a balance in an unusual direction (for example income with a debit balance).
  • Suspense and clearing accounts that are not empty.
  • Journals on weekends, round-sum and unusually large journals, month-end clustering.
Findings are prompts to ask questions, not accusations. Record your explanation in the period close checklist.

Tutorial: a 91-day treasury bill

  1. Settings first: accounts, WHT treatment, limits, approval.
  2. New Investment: class Treasury Bill, counterparty (Government), face value, price or discount rate, start and maturity dates, day count. The schedule previews.
  3. Submit and approve; the purchase is posted (bank credit, investment debit).
  4. At month end press Accrue in the portfolio screen (or let the month-end job do it if auto accrue is on): accrued income is posted.
  5. Seven days before maturity the portfolio shows it in Upcoming, and a notification rule on event investment_maturing can alert the treasury team.
  6. At maturity create the Maturity transaction, or Roll over to open a new draft with the matured amount.
  7. If you made a mistake, Cancel the newest transaction first, then amend it; the position is rebuilt from what is still posted.

Portfolio screen

Carrying value, cost, unrealised result, income earned, tax, yield; allocation by group, type and counterparty against limits; maturity ladder; upcoming events; holdings table with drill-down. API: GET /api/investments/portfolio, POST /api/investments/accrue, POST /api/investments/{name}/accrue | rollover.

Known limits. Bond premium and discount amortisation by effective interest rate is not implemented. A fair-value reserve (FVOCI) is not recycled to profit on disposal. Test results are from automated tests on SQLite; check an accrual against your own calculation on a small real position before relying on it.

23Support desk and service levels

Tickets with owners and clocks. Screen: Support Desk (#/support). Records: Support Ticket (with replies), Service Level Agreement (with priority targets), Support Team (with members), Support Category, Canned Reply, Support Settings.

How the clocks work

  • Each priority (Low, Medium, High, Urgent) has a response and a resolution target in hours or minutes.
  • Targets count in working time: working days, working hours, holidays and time zone from the agreement, or 24x7.
  • Agreement choice: customer-specific beats category-specific beats the default agreement.
  • Status Waiting on Customer pauses the clock; the due time moves by the paused period when work resumes.
  • SLA states: On track, At risk, Met, Breached, Paused. A job runs every minute to refresh them.
  • One escalation per breach goes to the agreement's escalate to user, otherwise the Support Settings escalation user; notification events support_sla_at_risk and support_sla_breached can alert people.
  • Resolved tickets close automatically after auto close days (daily job); a customer can reopen; satisfaction can be recorded.

Assignment

A Support Team assigns by Nobody (the team picks), Round robin or Least loaded. Source of a ticket: Phone, Email, WhatsApp, Portal, Walk-in, Internal. Requester: Customer, Employee, Other.

Set it up in ten minutes

  1. Support Settings: default priority, default team, auto-close days, escalation user.
  2. Create Support Categories.
  3. Create a Support Team and add members (agents) with the assignment rule.
  4. Create the default Service Level Agreement: working days and hours, holidays, time zone, targets per priority.
  5. Optionally a stricter SLA for a key customer.
  6. Add Canned Replies for common answers.
  7. Create a Notification Rule on support_sla_breached to SMS the team lead.

Daily work

The Support Desk shows KPIs (open, due soon, breached, first-response time, resolution time), the queue, workload by agent, ageing, trend and categories. On a ticket use Reply (POST /api/support/tickets/{name}/reply) and Assign (.../assign); the summary is GET /api/support/summary.

Not built: portal customers can raise tickets but cannot yet reply from the portal; inbound email-to-ticket is not included.

31Questions people ask

QuestionAnswer
I posted a wrong document. How do I fix it?Cancel it (the Cancel button needs the cancel right), then Amend or Duplicate it and submit the correct version. Cancelling removes its postings from the ledgers and keeps a log of what was removed; if other submitted documents depend on it the dialog lists them and offers Cancel All.
Who can approve?Whoever the document's workflow names (Settings → Workflows). With no workflow switched on nothing needs approval. Tick “the creator cannot approve” in the workflow if you want a maker-checker rule.
It says I can only receive goods through a Purchase Order.That restriction is gone (2.87). A Purchase Receipt or Delivery Note can be made with no order, or filled with Get Items From. If you still see it, you are on an older build: upgrade to 2.89.0.
Can I put several orders on one receipt or invoice?Yes. In Get Items From tick as many open orders (or receipts) as you like, as long as they are the same supplier or customer and the same currency.
The picker shows nothing.Only submitted, not closed documents with open quantity of the same party and currency are offered. Check the order is submitted and not already fully received or billed.
The first column of a table is something technical.It should not be: tables open with at most six useful columns. Add or hide columns with the Columns button on the table.
Can I stop overspending?Yes: budget control mode Warn or Block, with tolerance and override authority.
Why can't I change an item's valuation method?Because stock movements already exist; changing it would silently rewrite history.
Where is the Delivery Note's effect on accounts?It issues stock from the warehouse (checked against the cost floor so you do not sell below cost when that rule is on); revenue and tax are posted by the Sales Invoice.
A notification did not arrive.Check Notification Delivery (Retry or Dead), the worker, and use Send test.
How do I give a clerk access to only their own records?Add a row-level policy with equals_user on owner (framework manual, Security).
Can one company see another's data?No. Each company has its own database.
