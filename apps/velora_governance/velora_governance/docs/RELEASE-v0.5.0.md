# Velora Governance v0.5.0 — Governance Framework, Policies & Risk

## Added
- Governance Policy Types and controlled Policy versioning
- Policy owners, effective dates, review frequency and review calendar events
- Policy acknowledgements and review history
- Conflict of Interest Declarations with restricted personal/conflict information, review and mitigation tracking
- Related Party Interest register
- Governance Risk scoring (inherent and residual likelihood × impact)
- Governance Control register, effectiveness fields, testing dates and review automation
- Board Evaluation Templates with configurable questions and weights
- Board, Committee and Individual Director Evaluation records with mandatory response validation and weighted scoring
- Director Training and CPD records with expiry calendar events
- Board Succession Plans with candidate readiness, incumbent, skills gap and development actions
- Governance & Risk workspace
- Daily automation for policy reviews, control testing and training expiry

## Validation
- 28 automated tests passed
- 77 DocTypes registered and cross-validated
- SQLite integrated installation and lifecycle passed
- Integrated policy, conflict, related-party, risk, control, evaluation, training and succession records passed
- Policy/control/training overdue automation passed
- Package inspection and compilation passed

## Notes
Approval and policy adoption processes are configurable through the shared Velora Workflow Designer. No app-specific approval engine is implemented.
