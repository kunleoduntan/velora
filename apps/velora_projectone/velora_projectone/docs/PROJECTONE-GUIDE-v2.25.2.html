Welcome to ProjectOne

A project is a promise made to other people. ProjectOne helps you keep it, from the first business case to the closing report: portfolios and programmes that show what matters, plans with real dependencies, resources that are neither idle nor overbooked, risks that are named before they bite, and budgets that come from the same ledger the accountants use.

Because ProjectOne lives inside Velora beside VeloraERP, a purchase, a timesheet or an invoice for the project is never copied by hand: it is the same record, seen from the project's side. Programme managers, project managers, team members and finance officers all read the same truth, and each sees what is theirs to act on.

This guide is written for each of them. Find your part in section 1, follow the tutorials once with a small real project, and you will be running with confidence.

2What ProjectOne is

ProjectOne manages the whole project life from business case to closure. It runs inside Velora next to VeloraERP, and its figures come from the same ledger the accountants use.

Portfolio and programmesPortfolios, programmes, business cases, benefits, stage gates, charters and stakeholders.
PlanningWork breakdown, tasks with four kinds of dependency and lags, milestones, baselines, deliverables, sprints and user stories for agile teams.
ResourcesResource profiles, skills, calendars, availability exceptions, allocations, capacity and timesheets.
Financial controlBudgets with lines and changes, commitments, actual costs, revenue forecasts and recognition, performance obligations, snapshots.
GovernanceRisks, issues, change requests, corrective actions, quality inspections, decisions, meetings, documents and closure.
IntelligenceEleven dashboards, executive and manager workspaces, programme critical-path schedule, risk simulation and cost and resource position.

Rules that run through the product

  • One Project record. Every app that names a project (budgets, journal lines, HR documents) links to the same Project. There is no second project list.
  • Approval is a workflow you draw. ProjectOne never decides who approves; your administrator does that in the Workflow Designer, per organisation.
  • Permissions follow the Velora pattern used by every app: read, write, create, delete and the document rights (export, print, email, share) per role.
  • Money stays in its own currency. Totals are shown per currency and never converted silently.

3Installing and upgrading

ProjectOne and VeloraERP belong together: each requires the other.

pip install velora==1.15.5 velora_erp==2.94.1 velora_projectone==2.25.2   # or the supplied wheels
velora upgrade                                                              # applies the schema and the patches
velora doctor
  1. Stop the service, install the three wheels together, run velora upgrade, start the service.
  2. The install creates a Projects workspace in the sidebar and registers ProjectOne's screens.
  3. Coming from an earlier VeloraERP? Its Finance module had its own Project record. The upgrade patch adopt_projectone_projects carries those projects over: the name becomes the Title, the budget becomes the Approved Budget, Planned becomes Draft, Active becomes In Progress and the Project Manager is filled from the record's owner. Review them once and set the real manager and currency.
The patch fills only blank values and can be run again safely. Projects that already have ProjectOne values are left alone.

4Roles and permissions

ProjectOne uses the same permission rows as every Velora app. Give people roles in Users; change what a role may do in Role Permissions.

RoleWhat it can do
System ManagerEverything, including delete and set-up.
Project ManagerCreate, change and delete every project record; export, print, email and share.
Project UserCreate and change records in their projects, export and print; cannot delete.
AuditorRead, report, export and print only.
Finance ManagerFinancial-control documents where finance owns the figures.
Project Finance IntegrationThe integration service account that posts source figures.

A person who can fill a Link field (for example choosing a Customer on a project) but not open that list still sees the choices in the picker; a role that may not read the target at all sees none.

5Approvals are workflows

Nothing in ProjectOne is blocked by a built-in approval. If no workflow is switched on for a document, it simply saves; when one is on, it alone decides who approves.

  1. Open Workflows and create one for the document, for example Project Budget Change, Project Change Request, Project Timesheet or Project Corrective Action.
  2. Name the states, the role that may act at each, and tick “the creator cannot approve” for a maker-checker rule.
  3. Switch it on. People see waiting items in the approval inbox and in the mobile app's Approvals tab.

6Tutorial: create a project

  1. Open Projects → Project → New. Give the Title, a Project Code (it is upper-cased and becomes the record's name), the Project Manager, the Currency, the dates and the Approved Budget.
  2. Choose the Programme or Portfolio if there is one, and the Cost Centre or Business Unit so finance can report by them.
  3. Save. Status starts as Draft; move it to Open and In Progress as work begins.
  4. Add a Project Charter and Project Stakeholders from the project's connections.
The progress percent must be between 0 and 100, the finish cannot be before the start, and the budget cannot be negative. The form tells you which one to fix.

7Plan the work

  • Project WBS breaks the scope down; Project Task carries dates, hours, an assignee and a status. A task's finish cannot be before its start.
  • Task Dependency joins two tasks as Finish to Start, Start to Start, Finish to Finish or Start to Finish, with a lag in days.
  • Project Milestone, Project Deliverable and Project Baseline record promises and the plan you will measure against.
  • Agile teams use Project Sprint and Project User Story; the Project Schedule Workspace shows the schedule and critical path.

8Resources and timesheets

  1. Create a Resource Calendar (working weekdays such as 0,1,2,3,4 and a list of holiday dates) and mark one as the default.
  2. Create a Resource Profile per person, consultant, contractor or equipment with a daily capacity.
  3. Allocate people to projects with Project Resource Allocation (dates, hours, percentage and rates).
  4. People record time on a Project Timesheet; the Enterprise Capacity Workspace shows who is over or under loaded.

9Budgets, costs and revenue

Budgets are planned in ProjectOne; actual cost and recognised revenue are read from the Account Ledger, so ProjectOne and the accountants always agree.

  1. Create a Project Budget with Project Budget Lines by cost category. Later changes go through a Project Budget Change so there is a trail.
  2. In VeloraERP, tag expense and income lines with the project (the Project field on journal and invoice lines). The posting engine writes it to the ledger.
  3. Open the Project Financial Control dashboard: budget, commitments, actual cost, recognised revenue and forecast, each in the project's own currency.
If a project is in one currency and its ledger rows in another, ProjectOne reports the mismatch instead of converting.

10Risks, issues, changes and corrective action

  • Project Risk scores probability (1 to 5) times impact. To feed the simulation, also fill the best, likely and worst delay in working days and the best, likely and worst cost.
  • Project Issue and Project Change Request record problems and scope changes; approval follows the workflow you drew.
  • Project Corrective Action is created from an exception on the manager workbench: give it a title, an owner and a due date. It is saved as a Draft for its owner and sent to approval when a workflow exists.

11Programme intelligence

Open a Programme and use Programme Intelligence. Everything is calculated from your real tasks, dependencies, risks, allocations and ledger, read with your own permissions; you only choose the programme and the assumptions.

ToolWhat you get
Calendar scheduleStart and finish dates, float and the critical path using the project's working days and holidays.
Risk simulationA repeatable simulation (100 to 10,000 runs, a seed you choose) giving likely and pessimistic finish and cost.
Cost and resource positionBudget, actual and forecast by currency, and each person's demand against capacity.
Scenario comparisonCompare alternative plans side by side; advisory only, nothing is applied.
With no tasks or no risk figures the answers say so rather than inventing numbers.

12Dashboards and workspaces

Eleven dashboards: Portfolio Executive, Project Delivery, Schedule and Critical Path, Resource and Capacity, Project Financial Control, Commercial and Contracts, Risk and Governance, Agile Delivery, Quality and Deliverables, Programme Performance and Benefits Realisation.

  • Portfolio Executive Workspace for executives.
  • Project Manager Workbench for daily work: what is late, over budget or waiting for you.
  • Project Schedule Workspace and Enterprise Capacity Workspace for planners.

13The mobile app

The ProjectOne mobile app signs in with your Velora account (and your authenticator code if you use one).

  • Home: portfolio snapshot, data quality and source health.
  • Projects: search the list and open a project.
  • Approvals: the items waiting for you.
  • Timesheets: a quick hours check.
  • More: company, appearance (light, dark or system, five colour themes) and sign out.

What you see is what your roles allow; the server decides every request.

16Limits you should know

  • Risk simulation uses the risk figures you enter; with none it reports no exposure.
  • Forecast cost equals the cost posted so far until you enter Forecast Cost at Completion on the project.
  • Dates and numbers follow the company's Date & Number Format setting in lists and reports; the date pickers in forms follow your browser's own setting.
  • Procurement, sales, stock and asset figures come from the Project field on Purchase Orders, Sales Orders, Stock Entries (material issues) and Fixed Assets; invoices and receipts are tied to a project through journal lines.
  • The mobile app needs a network connection; it does not work offline.
